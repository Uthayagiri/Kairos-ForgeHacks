## 1. Executive Summary & Core Root Cause

### 🔍 The Core Root Cause of the Recall Difference
The large discrepancy between the reported **$98.56\%$ phishing recall** of the live production model and the **$70.02\%$ phishing recall** of the retrained model is caused by a **Train/Test Split Alignment Mismatch**:

1. **How Production Model v2.1.0 Was Originally Trained & Evaluated (`scratch/deploy_candidate_a4.py`):**
   * The production model was trained on an 80/20 domain split generated by:
     $$\text{GroupShuffleSplit}(n\_splits=1, test\_size=0.20, random\_state=42)$$
   * This allocated $7,111$ samples ($2,520$ domains) to the **training partition** and $1,799$ samples ($631$ domains) to its **test partition**.
   * On its **true held-out test split ($N=1,799$)**, Production Model v2.1.0 achieves:
     * **Recall:** **$94.21\%$** ($374 / 397$ attacks detected, $23$ false negatives)
     * **Accuracy:** **$94.55\%$**
     * **Precision:** **$83.30\%$** ($374 / 449$)
     * **FPR:** **$5.35\%$** ($75 / 1,402$)
     * **ROC-AUC:** **$0.9819$**

2. **How Candidate Experiments Evaluated the Models (`GroupKFold` Fold 1):**
   * Candidate experiments partitioned the dataset using:
     $$\text{GroupKFold}(n\_splits=5)$$
   * This allocated $1,782$ samples ($608$ domains) to Fold 1.
   * **The Evaluation Overlap:**
     * **$84.01\%$ ($1,497$ out of $1,782$)** of all samples in GroupKFold Fold 1 were actually part of the **TRAINING SET** of the pre-trained Production Model v2.1.0!
     * **$91.58\%$ ($370$ out of $404$)** of the phishing attack samples in GroupKFold Fold 1 had already been seen by Production Model v2.1.0 during its training!
   * Therefore, evaluating the pre-trained Production Model v2.1.0 on GroupKFold Fold 1 measured **in-sample training memorization ($98.56\%$ recall)** rather than out-of-sample generalization.

3. **Why the Retrained Model Achieved $70.02\%$ on GroupKFold Fold 1:**
   * The retrained baseline was trained on the GroupKFold training partition ($7,128$ samples) and evaluated on a **$100\%$ genuine held-out fold** ($1,782$ samples from $608$ unseen domains).
   * Because GroupKFold partitions domains into 5 equal buckets without shuffling seed alignment to the original split, out-of-sample domain generalization on Fold 1 without threshold tuning drops to $70.02\%$ at threshold $0.50$ (and recovers to $83.93\%$ at threshold $0.30$).

## 3. Summary & Recommended Action

1. **The "98.56% Recall" was an Artifact of Train/Test Domain Overlap Across Split Strategies:**
   * Evaluating the pre-trained production model against a different cross-validation fold partition resulted in evaluating the model on samples it had trained on.
   * On its true out-of-sample held-out domain split, the production model's genuine generalization recall is **$94.21\%$** (with accuracy $94.55\%$, precision $83.30\%$).
2. **Standardizing the Candidate Evaluation Framework:**
   * Future candidate evaluations should evaluate both the Production Model and Candidate Models on the exact same fixed out-of-sample test split (`GroupShuffleSplit` test partition, $N=1,799$) to ensure rigorous, apples-to-apples generalization testing.
